# Session recaps

Risk Desk → Share recap opens a standalone native dialog. Passport remains a separate broader-record artifact. Select a dated recap, background, and Story (1080×1920), Feed (1080×1350), or Square (1080×1080). The actual generated PNG is also the preview. Download is always available once rendering completes; Web Share appears only when the browser accepts that PNG file. No social account connection or automatic posting is implied.

## Accounting and dates

- Consume only the already owner/account-scoped complete history. Group known same-opening-fill partial exits before choosing a day or region. Counts are trade entries, not certified flat-to-flat positions; unmatched rows are reported trades. Win rate includes breakevens in the denominator and does not round a losing record to 100%.
- Keep cents in integer arithmetic. Reject duplicates, mixed accounts, unsupported/unknown currencies, invalid cents/dates and malformed/partial timing evidence. Do not change risk, existing broker-cash summary calculations, the source ledger, or Passport calculations.
- Tradovate uses **after-fee cash P&L** as the headline: selected-window trade postings plus signed posted fees, excluding funding and unrealized P&L. The source gross ledger stays immutable. Require reconciled owner/account cash evidence and the exact saved **reported-window** trade fingerprint; unrelated older history outside that window must not disable a covered recap. Selected groups must lie inside the recap window; bidirectional trade-posting correspondence includes multiplicity and exact timestamp/cents/market-root granularity. This is not a fill-ID or expiry-level linkage. Accept the reconciled snapshot through its exact as-of timestamp even before the review session or UTC day ends; reject future-dated rows. Fees can relate to carried/open positions, so no per-trade fee allocation or net win rate is invented. **Win rate** retains grouped gross trade outcomes. The fee breakdown, timestamp and accounting explanation remain in What’s included, not on the image. If Tradovate fees cannot reconcile, block the net export with a sync instruction in the composer; never silently substitute gross or zero fees. Other verified USD providers retain their reported-P&L disclosure; samples remain unmistakably synthetic.
- A daily bucket uses a group's final observed exit date (UTC with validated timestamps, otherwise the coherent imported date). Whole groups crossing days remain together on the final date. Date-only histories do not gain invented intraday timestamps.
- Named regional recaps require *every* contributing entry and exit to be in the same dated, half-open review window. New York: 09:30–16:00 America/New_York. London: 08:00 Europe/London until 09:30 America/New_York. Asia: 09:00 Asia/Tokyo until 08:00 Europe/London. IANA zones handle DST, including weeks when US and UK changes differ. These are review windows, not exchange calendars or assertions a market was open. Off-window or cross-region groups remain Daily-only.
- Pure demo histories carry an unavoidable Sample data mark in every format. Mixed sample/imported histories cannot be shared.

## Hot streak

Hot streak is consecutive net-green UTC trading days for the selected account through the recap date, not consecutive winning trades. Derive it from validated whole-day recaps and the same reconciled cash evidence used by after-fee headlines. Regional cards inherit the whole day's streak, not a favorable isolated session. Red or after-fee breakeven resets it; idle days and deposits do not increment or reset it. Cash-only activity without a reconciled trading-day recap and unknown fee days stop continuity instead of being skipped. If a prior reset cannot be established, show a qualified lower bound such as `3+ DAY HOT STREAK`, never an invented exact start. Hide zero/unavailable streaks and never add a real-account streak to unsupported/sample records. Today's value is a mutable sync snapshot, not proof that the trader has finished trading. Use number-first `3 DAY HOT STREAK` (or `1 DAY HOT STREAK`) as a prominent blue typographic line under the result; no flame emoji, rank, filled badge or predictive claim.

## Identity, artwork and lifecycle

Saved username/avatar are read through the existing owner-guarded profile provider. Identity-enabled exports wait until that profile finishes loading; a failed load offers retry or explicit identity hiding, and a missing saved photo is disclosed. One-off owner exports must supply the actual saved/supplied profile photo rather than silently passing a null avatar. Hiding identity removes both from pixels and accessible preview text. Filenames never include usernames, original photo names, or account IDs. Background photos themselves are user-controlled and may reveal identity.

Every format centers the result and symmetric stat columns, then stacks the actual COVA workspace wordmark (`/media/wordmark-options/cova-wordmark-option-3-sleek-cropped.png`) above a smaller muted website line. Do not substitute the C-only mobile icon or a typed wordmark. Real Tradovate cards omit gross/before-fee labels, unavailable-fee/net-cash lines, and footer time. The top date stays; samples keep their unavoidable Sample data footer.

The approved Option B uses self-hosted Space Grotesk for this export only, with one cohesive bold signed amount. Grouping separators, two decimal places and sign/loss color are preserved; the entire amount must fit without truncation. The geometric amount, uppercase recap title, strong blue `3 DAY HOT STREAK` line and centered footer match the approved source-rendered preview. Font loading blocks export rather than silently producing fallback typography. Market labels use Cova cobalt. Bundled photos receive a mild contrast/highlight lift and edge vignette before any identity or text is drawn; Plain and custom-upload photo grading stay unchanged.

Bundled city artwork is illustrative. Changing artwork never changes the selected financial session. JPG/PNG/WebP uploads are limited to 8 MB / 40 decoded megapixels, downsampled to a maximum dimension of 1920, re-encoded to JPEG to remove metadata, and kept only in the current dialog. No upload endpoint or remote image URL is used.

Owner/account changes dismiss the composer. Pixel-input changes invalidate export eligibility immediately. Cash-only updates, cross-tab storage changes and focus revalidation reread owner-scoped evidence and invalidate stale fee artifacts without changing the trades. Rendering uses cancellation and exact input keys; a completed artifact must also own a still-live object URL. Closing, replacing a render, or changing scope revokes URLs. Late photo decoding cannot replace a newer background choice. Already handed-off downloaded/shared files cannot be recalled.
