# Third-party provenance: Brand Orbs

Cova vendors the complete authored `brand-orbs-v2.html` specimen as the canonical source for the X, Instagram, and Email dot-orb renderers used in the landing footer.

- Component: Brand Orbs
- Author attribution in the source: Meng To
- Distribution/source: ThreeUI, https://threeui.com/ui-elements/brand-orbs/x
- Source commit identifier published by ThreeUI: SHA-256 `c2733de8cf7b`
- Upstream inspiration: Thinking Orbs by Jakub Antalik, https://github.com/Jakubantalik/thinking-orbs

The Cova adapter mounts only the requested variants in `allow-scripts` sandboxed iframes, pauses offscreen/hidden work, and preserves reduced-motion behavior. The full source remains unmodified as provenance; runtime adaptation occurs in `BrandOrbs.tsx`.
